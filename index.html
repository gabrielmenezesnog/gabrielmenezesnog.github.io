A little about myself

My name is Gabriel de Menezes Nogueira, I'm a Full Stack Developer from Curitiba, Paraná - Brazil. I have 5 years of experience building web and mobile applications for national and international projects, working with React, React Native, Next.js and TypeScript on the front-end and Node.js, Express and Java Spring Boot on the back-end.

Today I work at Volta Health (USA) with an international team, and I'm studying Systems Analysis and Development at IFPR. Outside of work I founded and built Health & Lifts, an AI-powered fitness platform developed end to end: from the mobile app to cloud infrastructure, store release and production operations.
